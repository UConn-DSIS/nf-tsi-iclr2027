Workshop Overview

Three critical frontiers remain open in time series AI — and this workshop addresses all three simultaneously.

🧩
Multimodal Time Series Learning
Real-world temporal data rarely appear as clean numerical sequences. Clinical time series accompany free-text notes and medical images; financial signals correlate with news and reports. Multimodal integration can dramatically expand what is learnable from time series.
🤖
Agentic & Self-Evolving AI for Time Series
Beyond prediction, real-world temporal intelligence requires agency: retrieving historical analogues, invoking tools, running iterative experiments, and collaborating with human experts. Evolutionary agents and recursive self-improvement (RSI) mechanisms hold transformative potential.
🌍
Translating SOTA to Real-World Applications
A substantial gap persists between cutting-edge research and practical deployment in healthcare, Earth science, energy, and industrial IoT. This workshop provides a dedicated platform for time series researchers, domain scientists, and industry stakeholders to forge collaborations.
Topics of Interest

NF-TSI welcomes submissions presenting novel methods, benchmarks, and application results relevant to multimodal and agentic AI for time series.

🔀
Multimodal Time Series Learning
Cross-modal fusion, alignment, and transference; contrastive and self-supervised multimodal pretraining; modality-missing robustness.
🏗️
Foundation Models for Time Series
Large-scale pretraining, zero-shot and few-shot transfer, tokenization and patching strategies, universal representations, benchmark evaluation.
💬
LLM/VLM-Based Temporal Modeling
Prompt learning and instruction tuning, semantic space alignment, language-guided forecasting and anomaly detection, vision-model-based temporal data analysis.
⚙️
Agentic & Self-Evolving AI
Temporal workflow agents, RAG for time series, autonomous data analysis, evolutionary agents and RSI for time series, human-in-the-loop systems, multi-agent collaboration.
🧠
Time Series Reasoning & Grounding
QA over temporal data, temporal reasoning with language models, event-aware forecasting, counterfactual temporal reasoning, interpretability.
🔗
Causal Discovery & Inference
Granger causality, structural causal models for temporal data, interventional time series modeling.
🛡️
Robust & Trustworthy Temporal AI
Distribution shift, domain adaptation, uncertainty quantification, fairness and bias in temporal models, reliability of agentic temporal systems.
🗺️
Spatiotemporal & Graph-Structured
Spatiotemporal forecasting, image-based forecasting, traffic and mobility prediction, graph neural networks for temporal data.
📊
Benchmarks, Datasets & Evaluation
New datasets, evaluation protocols, leakage-aware assessment, reproducibility and standardization for multimodal/agentic time series.
🌡️
Applications
Healthcare and biomedicine (EEG, fMRI, clinical monitoring), climate and earth systems, energy and power grids, finance and AIOps, IoT and cyber-physical systems, scientific discovery.

Call for Papers

All submissions undergo double-blind peer review via OpenReview.

Submission Tracks

  • Full Papers — 4–9 pages (excluding references and appendices). For novel research contributions.
  • Tiny Papers — 2 pages (excluding references and appendices). For late-breaking results, datasets, and position papers.

Review Criteria

  • Relevance to workshop topics
  • Technical quality and novelty
  • Clarity of writing and presentation
  • Potential impact
